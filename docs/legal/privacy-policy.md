# Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-03

{{BRAND_NAME}} operates "Teranga", a prototype built for the World Bank x Hack-Nation "Small AI for Development" hackathon. It is not a commercial service.

## What we collect
- **Phone numbers** of people who message or call the service. Twilio processes them to deliver messages and calls. The service itself remembers a conversation using a salted hash of the number, not the number.
- **Messages and questions** you send to the WhatsApp or SMS number.
- **Voice recordings and transcripts** of answers recorded by a tourism operator, and of voice notes you send.
- **Feedback you choose to share.** Voice or text feedback is kept only for your own use and deleted within 24 hours unless you reply SHARE.
- **Simple usage counts**, such as how many questions were asked per topic.

## How we use it
To answer visitor questions with pre-approved answers, to send tourism operators notifications (a summary after a recording call and an optional weekly digest), and to let a family helper review recordings. We do not use your data for advertising.

## Who processes data for us
Twilio (messaging and voice), ElevenLabs (speech-to-text and text-to-speech), Lovable Cloud (hosting and database), and AI language models used through Lovable AI for translation. They process data only to provide the service.

We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

## Retention and your choices
Recordings and transcripts are kept while the prototype is running and are deleted on request. Reply STOP to any SMS to stop receiving messages. To ask for access or deletion, contact {{CONTACT_EMAIL}}.

## Children
The service is not directed to children under 13.
